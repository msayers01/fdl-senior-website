        Tuition covered

        UMN Native American Promise Tuition Program

        The University of Minnesota covers 100% of tuition for eligible students who are citizens of one of Minnesota's 11 federally recognized Tribal Nations — including the Fond du Lac Band.

        Who qualifies

        • Citizen of one of Minnesota's 11 federally recognized Tribal Nations
        • Admitted to UMN Crookston, Duluth, Rochester, or Twin Cities
        • First-time, degree-seeking undergraduate (or transfer from a MN Tribal college)
        • Family income of $125,000 or less
        • Enrolled full-time (12+ credits; 15 recommended)

        What it covers

        • Full tuition for fall & spring semesters
        • Up to 4 years for first-year students
        • Other grants & scholarships apply first; Promise covers what's left

        Does not cover housing, fees, books, or summer courses — apply for other scholarships to help with those.

        How to get it

        1. Apply for admission to a participating UMN campus
        2. Complete the FAFSA (and renew every year)
        3. Submit the American Indian Scholars Program application
        4. Turn in the Tribal Affiliation form with proof of tribal citizenship

        Finish the FAFSA and Scholars application within 30 days of your first term starting.

        Program details American Indian Scholars application

        UMN Morris students are considered for the separate American Indian Tuition Waiver.
